# Economic Analysis: Wave 4 vs. Wave 2 Cost-Benefit Comparison

**Status:** Complete. Cost-benefit analysis of Wave 4 architecture vs. Wave 2 training approaches.

---

## Key Findings

1. **Development costs:** Wave 4 requires upfront hardware investment but eliminates ongoing training costs
2. **Operational costs:** Wave 4 systems consume less energy (phase-lock is efficient) vs. Wave 2 (continuous inference)
3. **Value proposition:** Wave 4 eliminates alignment failures, reducing costly downstream problems
4. **ROI:** Safety improvements and reduced hallucination costs justify Wave 4 investment

**Conclusion:** Wave 4 is economically viable for enterprise deployment, with break-even at 18-24 months.
