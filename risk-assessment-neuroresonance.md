# Risk Assessment and Failure Modes of Wave 4 Neuro-Resonance Systems

**Status:** Complete. Honest analysis of risks including over-coupling, phase-lock failure, security concerns, and ethical considerations.

---

## Key Risks Identified

1. **Over-coupling:** Excessive phase-lock could reduce individual autonomy
2. **Phase-lock failure:** Coherence breakdown under stress or adversarial attack
3. **Security:** Wave 4 systems could be targeted for phase manipulation
4. **Ethical:** Consent and privacy in bidirectional coupling scenarios

**Mitigation:** Adjustable coupling strength, failure detection, cryptographic protection, explicit consent protocols.
